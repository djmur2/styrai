# StyrAI Evidence-Led Product Roadmap

## Demonstrated now

- a fictional employee workbench, attached enforcement overlay and central
  command centre connected by one end-to-end execution run;
- deterministic local model drafting only after the outbound input is permitted;
- external action held for an independent reviewer, then executed separately
  through a receipt-bound, idempotent local tool adapter;
- a blocked public-AI/credential route in which provider and tool invocation never
  occur, with automatic incident and DLP automation evidence;
- a correlated execution-evidence export spanning runtime event, model fixture,
  policy, approval, authorisation and final outcome;
- one command centre across inventory, model monitoring, data protection,
  controls, automation and incidents;
- deterministic allow, redact, approval-required and block decisions;
- secret detection, confidential-data redaction and raw-prompt minimisation;
- exact model/version and intended-use passports;
- quality, drift and latency thresholds with automated containment;
- silent model-version detection and revalidation gating;
- shadow-AI discovery and blocked unknown destinations;
- one- and two-person action approval with separation of duties;
- hash-chained decisions, automation runs, incident resolution and evidence packs;
- technical framework mappings for EU AI Act, DORA, ISO 42001 and NIST AI RMF;
- automated acceptance tests and a resettable financial-services demonstration.

The exit condition is no longer “build a dashboard.” It is a qualified prospect
providing one real workflow and accepting a paid, bounded design-partner pilot.

## First paid design partner

1. Tenant isolation, authentication and durable policy/evidence storage.
2. Enterprise identity and delegated agent/workload identity.
3. One report-only model-gateway or application connector.
4. Customer-owned inventory/passport reconciliation and review workflow.
5. Customer-side DLP/redaction with metadata-only central evidence.
6. Production metrics and thresholds agreed with the model owner.
7. Signed, expiring, non-replayable action authorisation.
8. One real human-approval and outcome-reconciliation workflow.
9. Ticketing/SIEM/GRC incident and evidence integration.
10. Threat model, privacy/security review, resilience, rollback and recovery tests.

## Repeatability after the first pilot

- connector kit for major model gateways and agent frameworks;
- scheduled shadow-AI and unregistered-model discovery;
- provider, region, retention and concentration-risk monitoring;
- change-impact graph across model, prompt, tool, data, provider and policy;
- continuous evaluation signals linked to model passports and incidents;
- configurable technical control packs and customer-owned mappings;
- customer-hosted evidence or enforcement for strict data boundaries;
- multi-tenant operating dashboards, support runbooks and service metrics.

## Explicitly deferred

- training or hosting foundation models;
- replacing IAM, DLP, SIEM, GRC or independent model validation;
- autonomous production policy changes based on regulatory news;
- retaining raw prompts or hidden model reasoning by default;
- autonomous credit, insurance pricing, investment advice or trading;
- “fully compliant” or certification claims.

## Feature admission rule

A feature enters delivery only when it links to a paid-pilot acceptance criterion,
an observed customer workflow, a demonstrated security/resilience need, or a
specific customer-reviewed obligation. New regulation creates a versioned review
task—not an automatic production-policy change.
