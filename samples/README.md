# StyrAI synthetic upload pack

These files contain no real customer, patient or employee information. They are designed for the local upload-driven governance demonstration.

1. Upload `synthetic-credit-decisions.csv` or `synthetic-clinical-summaries.json` in the AI Control Room.
2. Upload `stricter-company-ai-policy.md`, or paste its two enforceable clauses into the policy box.
3. Review the extracted draft rules and activate them when prompted.
4. Run the assessment.

The samples intentionally include:

- rows where all applicable regulatory control proxies pass;
- rows with a potential regulatory control gap;
- rows that pass the regulatory screen but require manual review under a stricter company policy; and
- an unparsed policy clause that remains visible for human review rather than being silently enforced.

Every result is a synthetic control evaluation, not a legal opinion or certification.
